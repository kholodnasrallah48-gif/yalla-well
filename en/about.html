About

Why we made Yalla Well.

Most diet apps give everyone the same numbers, never ask about health conditions or medicine times, and stop once you reach a target weight. We wanted an app that works all of that out and stays with you after the goal: healthy habits and easy follow-up for years.

In plain words

The app talks the way people talk, and its Arabic adjusts for men and women. English too.

Health changes the plan

We take health conditions and medicines into account in training, food and timing, instead of leaving them in a side note.

Your data, your call

Without an account everything stays on your phone. An account is optional, so your data can come back on a new phone.

Not a doctor

We help you organise and keep track; medical decisions stay with your doctor. The report makes that conversation easier.

The captain

The champ from our logo lives in the app.

The captain shows up on every screen doing something that fits it: cheering on Today, eating on Food and lifting on Training. Each day he says something about how your day is going.

Who it is for

Yalla Well is made for

  • Anyone who wants to lose, keep or gain weight with varied food, without a short-term diet
  • People who train at the gym, at home, or a bit of both
  • People living with a long-term health condition and taking medicines and supplements
