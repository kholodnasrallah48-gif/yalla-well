Features

What the app does, in detail.

The app starts from your body, your goal and your health. Every condition or medicine you add changes your food, your training and your timings, so no two plans look the same.

FoodEating outTrainingYour healthFor womenReportsProgressStreakThe Captain

Food

Any food, with its calories.

Search by name, recipes that adjust to what's left of your day, and an alert when a food doesn't suit you.

Search by name

More than 350 foods, each with its weight, calories, protein, carbs and fat.

Ideas that fit what's left

The next meal is suggested from the calories you have left, not a fixed number.

Lighter recipes

151 recipes from Arab kitchens, with amounts that change with your calories.

Alerts and swaps

If a food doesn't suit your health or you're allergic to it, a red alert shows up with lighter swaps.

Food allergies

Asked the first time: nuts, milk, eggs, fish, wheat or any other food by name. Recipes with it stay out of your suggestions, and logging it shows a red alert.

Your own plan

If a doctor or dietitian gave you a plan, enter its numbers and the app follows them instead of its own.

Barcode scanning for packaged food is in the phone app, and foods that aren't listed can be found in public food databases.

Photo apps guess the portion from a picture. In a test of 4 well-known photo apps, meals came out about 250 to 345 kcal under the real amount (an NIH research team, NUTRITION 2026, not yet peer reviewed). Read the news

Eating out still counts.

Your health

The plan knows your health.

Medicines timed around your meals, a lighter workout on a rough day, and lab results and a doctor report ready whenever you need them.

Medicines with meals

Each one with its dose and time, and where it sits around food: before, with or after. The phone app reminds you on time.

A rough day? The plan eases off

When energy is low, pain is bad and sleep was poor, today's workout turns into an easy recovery day on its own. If the day turns out fine, one tap brings it back.

Lab results, compared

The result and the date, with a reminder before the next test. 8 tests are tracked, and a PDF compares the last 3 results of each.

A report for your doctor

The last 4 weeks in a PDF: medicines taken, energy, pain and sleep, rough days, workouts and lab results. Print it or send it.

Yalla Well helps you organise and keep track. It doesn't diagnose, and decisions about medicine, food or training are made with your doctor.

For women

Your cycle and pregnancy, inside the plan.

Period and pregnancy tracking live in the same plan, so training and calories change with the day and the month.

Your cycle

Log the first day only, and the app works out your next period and fertile days from past dates.

Lighter first two days

On the first two days of your period, the workout gets lighter by itself. Feeling fine? One tap brings back the usual one.

Pregnancy

Your week and trimester. Calories with no deficit, and exercises that don't fit are swapped month by month.

Reports

Reports you can print.

A weekly report, a lab comparison and a report for your doctor. Each one is a PDF to print or send.

A week score from calories, protein, water and workouts, day by day, with what went well and what needs work.

Progress

The change, in numbers.

Weight alone doesn't tell the whole story, so the app also tracks measurements and body fat, and makes a photo of your week with its numbers.

Progress page

Your weight on a chart, measurements in cm, an estimated body-fat percentage and progress photos. The photos stay on your phone.

Progress photo

The camera opens inside a frame with your real numbers for the week: streak, workouts and days on target. Take the photo and share it with friends.

Streak

Miss a day? A freeze has it covered.

Every day within your calories adds to your streak. Every 7 days in a row earns a freeze, and if you miss a day, the freeze covers it and the streak keeps going.
