About

An app built for Egyptian food and real life.

Most diet and fitness apps don't know koshari, and never ask about your thyroid, rheumatoid arthritis or medicine times. Yalla Well was built to close that gap.

In Egyptian

The food actually on the table, the way people actually talk, and English too.

Health-aware

Chronic and autoimmune conditions and medicines change food, training and timing, not just a note.

Your data stays on your phone

No account, and no server of ours holding your health history.

Not a doctor

We help you organise and keep track; medical decisions stay with your doctor. The report makes that conversation easier.

The captain

The champ from our logo is on your side.

The captain shows up on every screen doing what you're doing: cheering on Today, eating on Food and lifting on Training. Every day he has something to say about your day.

And when a day goes well, he genuinely celebrates.

Who it is for

Yalla Well is made for

  • Anyone who wants to lose, keep or gain weight eating their everyday food
  • People who train at the gym, at home, or a bit of both
  • People living with a chronic or autoimmune condition and taking medicines and supplements
