One small step

Put the web version on your home screen

So it opens like any app, with the captain as its icon.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the web version in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button
  3. "Add to Home Screen"

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the web version in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu
  3. "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"

The web version has no reminders or barcode camera. Those come with the phone app.
