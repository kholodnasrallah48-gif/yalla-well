Features

Everything inside Yalla Well.

Food, training and health in one place, and each part talks to the others: your condition shapes your food and training, and your medicines shape your day.

FoodTrainingHealth and medicinesAnd also

Food

Real Egyptian food, honestly counted.

Your calorie, protein, carb and fat targets come from your age, height, weight, activity and goal.

Logging

  • 314 Egyptian and Arab foods, each with portion, calories and macros
  • Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks; a meal counts once it's ticked as eaten
  • Type a meal in words, like "2 eggs and baladi bread", and the app understands it
  • Your most used foods come first

If a food isn't listed

  • Online search in public food databases, saved to "My foods"
  • Barcode scanning for packaged food in the phone app

Warnings and ideas

  • A red warning for high salt, going over your fat or carb limit, or food that does not suit your condition
  • Meal ideas sized to what's left, lighter when you're close to your limit
  • 56 recipes by name, scaled to the calories you've got left
  • The day locks after midnight and is saved for your report

Training

A training week that fits you.

Nine plans with plain names, from 3 to 6 days, and every day of the week set your way.

Plans

  • Full body, upper and lower, push pull legs, glutes and legs, one muscle a day, and 5 days split between gym and home
  • Not sure? Three questions and we suggest one
  • Set each day to gym, home or rest, and choose the split for gym days

Exercises

  • 90 exercises with pictures and cues
  • A machine version with the machine named, and a free-weight version
  • Remove an exercise, or add your own to the day
  • A how-to video search on YouTube

At the gym

  • Type the weight, set reps with plus and minus
  • A rest timer that runs in real time
  • A 4-week program that steps up the load, alternating machine and free-weight weeks

Health and medicines

It understands your health, and adjusts to it.

Twelve conditions to pick from, plus 85 conditions, medicines and supplements the app recognises when you type them.

Conditions

  • Hashimoto's, Graves', rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis, lupus, type 1 and 2 diabetes, insulin resistance, PCOS, coeliac disease, MS, Crohn's and ulcerative colitis
  • Type anything else; if the app does not know it, three quick questions about food, training and warning signs
  • Food adjusts: warnings, foods to avoid with certain medicines, less protein for kidney problems
  • Training adjusts: lower intensity, gentler moves for painful joints, and a card of warning signs to stop

Medicines and supplements

  • Dose, amount and form: pill, drops, injection, sachet and more
  • Daily, every few days, set weekdays, weekly or monthly, with several times a day
  • On an empty stomach, with food or at bedtime, with a reminder each time
  • A warning when two must be taken apart, and a lighter workout the day after a weekly injection

Keeping track

  • A daily check-in on energy, pain and sleep, with an easier workout on rough days
  • The lab tests that matter for you, your results and when the next one is due
  • A PDF report for your doctor covering the last 4 weeks

And also

What makes every day easier.

Arabic and English

Switch any time; Arabic text adapts to you as a man or a woman.

Reminders

For meals, water, workouts, medicines and lab tests, worded differently each day.

Weekly report

Each day green or red, past weeks included, ready to print or share as a PDF.

Points and streaks

For staying between 75% and 110% of your calorie target, and for every finished workout.

The captain

The champ from our logo comments on your day and gives you a tour the first time.

Your way

Light or dark, a profile photo, and sounds you can turn off.
