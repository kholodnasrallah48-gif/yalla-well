Food

Only what you actually ate counts.

Calories and protein are set for your body and goal, and the food is real Egyptian food: ful, koshari, molokhia and mahshi.

  • Meals count when you tick them, not by the clock
  • Protein, carbs and fat for every food, with a warning for high salt or anything that doesn't suit your condition
  • Anything not on the list is looked up online

Training

Gym, home or rest. Every day, your call.

Pick one of nine plans, and every day comes ready with exercises, sets and reps.

  • A machine and a free-weight version of every exercise
  • Log weight and reps, with a rest timer
  • A program that steps up the load week by week

Health and medicines

It understands your health, and adjusts everything to it.

Hashimoto's, rheumatoid arthritis, diabetes, blood pressure and more, plus anything you type in yourself.

  • Medicine and supplement times with dose and frequency
  • Warning signs to stop training, based on your condition
  • Lab tracking and a PDF report for your doctor

Questions

Frequently asked

What is Yalla Well?

An app that counts calories from Egyptian food, plans your training week at the gym or at home, organises medicine and supplement times, and takes any health condition into account in all of it.

Does it replace my doctor?

No. Yalla Well helps you organise and keep track; decisions about medicine, diet and training are for you and your doctor.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, not on a server of ours. Details are on the privacy page.

Which phones does it work on?

The web version works now on any phone or computer. iPhone and Android apps are coming soon.

Does it cost anything?

The current version is a trial and has no subscription.
